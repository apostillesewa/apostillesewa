Navigating document attestation in Nepal has undergone a total digital transformation. The **Department of Consular Services** under the **Ministry of Foreign Affairs (MOFA)** now mandates all document attestation procedures to begin online through the **Nepal e-Attestation System (NEATS)**. 

Walk-in physical submissions without pre-approval, verified online receipts, and official appointment slots are strictly rejected at the Tripureshwor office.

Whether you are preparing academic certificates for abroad studies, vital statistics for family reunification, or corporate paperwork for international operations, this comprehensive guide covers the 2026 MOFA attestation system, pre-verification steps, fees, and submission protocols.

---

## What is the New NEATS MOFA Online Attestation System?

The **NEATS (Nepal e-Attestation System)** portal (`doc.nepalconsular.gov.np`) is the official digital infrastructure implemented by the Department of Consular Services to audit documents digitally before physical submission. 

### Core Features of NEATS:
* **Digital Identity Matching:** Cross-references passport and citizenship records with uploaded documents.
* **Streamlined Physical Processing:** Verification and fee payment occur online, drastically cutting down crowd wait times in Tripureshwor.
* **Real-time Document Tracking:** Track application status from submission to final stamping via Reference Number.
* **Fraud Elimination:** Implements digital QR codes on final attested stickers for immediate embassy validation.

## Mandatory Pre-Verification Requirements (Before MOFA)

MOFA is the **final local authority** in Nepal’s legalization chain. Before submitting scans on the NEATS portal, your original documents must hold specific prior attestation stamps based on their issuing authority:

| Document Type | Prerequisite Verifications Needed |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Academic Certificates** (SEE / NEB / +2) | Sanothimi Examination Controller Office / National Examinations Board |
| **University Degrees & Transcripts** | Respective University Dean Office / CDC Kirtipur |
| **Birth, Marriage, & Single Status** | Local Ward Office & Municipality Registration Office |
| **Police Clearance Certificate (PCC)** | Issued digitally via Nepal Police portal (`att.nepalconsular.gov.np`) |
| **Divorce Decrees / Judicial Records** | Authorized Law Book Management Board Translation |
| **Commercial Documents** | Nepal Chamber of Commerce / Department of Commerce |

> **Note:** Documents lacking valid prior ministry, department, or local government stamps will be rejected during the online verification stage.

## Step-by-Step MOFA Online Application Process (2026)

Follow this 6-step workflow to ensure your document gets approved on the first attempt:

### Step 1: Online Application & High-Resolution Uploads
1. Access the official portal: `doc.nepalconsular.gov.np`.
2. Select your category: *Personal, Educational, Legal, or Commercial*.
3. Input applicant profile details matching your **Passport** and **Citizenship Certificate** exactly.
4. Upload flat, full-color, high-resolution scans of the front and back of your original document, passport/citizenship copy, and applicant photo.

### Step 2: Consular Officer Audit & Verification
* Officers review the legibility, seals, and prior authority stamps on your uploaded scans.
* Application status will update to **"Approved"** or **"Rejected"**.
* If rejected due to blurry scans or missing intermediate stamps, fix the errors specified in the notification before re-submitting.

### Step 3: Online Fee Payment
* Upon approval, the digital payment voucher generates.
* The official government fee is **NPR 500 per document**.
* Pay electronically via integrated payment gateways (connectIPS, eSewa, Khalti) or generate a bank voucher for Global IME Bank payment.
* **Download and print the official paid revenue receipt.**

### Step 4: Book Your Tripureshwor Appointment
* Choose an available time slot and date for physical document verification.
* Select early slots if you have approaching visa deadlines or embassy submission dates.

### Step 5: Physical Verification at Department of Consular Services
On your appointment date, present the physical file at:
* **Location:** Department of Consular Services, Tripureshwor, Kathmandu.
* **Items to bring:**
  1. Original documents bearing prior stamps.
  2. Clear photocopies of all original documents.
  3. Printed NEATS Application Form.
  4. Printed Official Revenue Payment Voucher.
  5. Original Passport / Citizenship Certificate (or representative authorization).

### Step 6: QR Code Verification & MOFA Stamping
Consular officers scan the QR code on your printed application, match original documents with uploaded digital scans, and apply the official MOFA sticker and wet-ink stamp.

## MOFA Attestation for Overseas Nepalese & Representatives

If you are currently living abroad (UAE, Qatar, Saudi Arabia, Europe, USA, Australia, etc.), **you do not need to fly back to Nepal** to complete MOFA attestation. 

You can authorize an official service provider or designated representative to handle the entire digital verification, physical submission, and collection process in Kathmandu.

## Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)

### What is the official website for Nepal MOFA attestation?
The official portal for general document attestation is `doc.nepalconsular.gov.np`. Police Clearance Certificates are verified via `att.nepalconsular.gov.np`.

### What is the official government fee for MOFA attestation?
The government fee is **NPR 500 per document**, payable online through integrated gateways or Global IME Bank vouchers.

### Can someone else submit documents on my behalf at Tripureshwor?
Yes. Representatives can submit and collect documents on your behalf provided they carry your original documents, citizenship copy, and authorization.

### What is the next step after MOFA attestation for foreign visas?
After receiving the MOFA stamp, documents usually require translation (if applicable) and final legalization by the target country's Embassy or Consulate in Kathmandu or New Delhi.
