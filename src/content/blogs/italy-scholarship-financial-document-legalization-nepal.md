## Unlocking Fully Funded Italian Scholarships: The ISEE Parificato Financial Chain

Italy stands out as one of the most generous European destinations for Nepalese students, offering robust, fully funded **need-based regional scholarships** (such as DSU, ER.GO, EDISU, and ALiSEO). These scholarships frequently cover 100% of university tuition fees, provide free student housing, and grant a yearly cash stipend to cover living expenses.

However, to prove your financial eligibility to the Italian regional authorities, you must calculate your family’s economic index, known as the **ISEE Parificato**. 

To do this, Italian universities and Tax Assistance Centers (CAF) require your family’s financial and relationship records to be strictly legalized. While the Ministry of Foreign Affairs (MoFA) in Kathmandu regulates general processing, it will only stamp specific, structured civil documents for this purpose. Navigating this bureaucratic pipeline from the local ward level to the Italian consular network requires total precision.

---

## The Core Documents Required by MoFA & Italy

Depending on the specific region or prefecture (*Prefettura*) in Italy, requested verification records can slightly vary. However, **MoFA at Tripureshwor will only sign off on two primary foundational documents** for foreign scholarship evaluations:

1.  **Relationship Certificate (*Nata प्रमाणित*):** An official certificate proving the exact layout and composition of your immediate family unit living under your household.
2.  **Low Income Certificate (*आय प्रमाणित*):** A formal document clearly declaring the total gross annual income generated by every working member of your household.

Both certificates must be presented in a specific, standardized format issued directly by your government authorities.

## The Step-by-Step Legalization Ladder

To ensure your financial papers are legally recognized by the Italian regional scholarship boards, you must climb an explicit, unskippable administrative ladder:
[Local Ward Office] ➔ [Municipality Section Officer] ➔ [MoFA Tripureshwor] ➔ [Certified Italian Translation] ➔ [Italian Diplomatic Network]

### Step 1: The Ward Office Origin (Local Level)
Your process must begin at your local **Ward Office (*Ward Karyalaya*)**. You must present your family's citizenship cards, land tax records (*Lalpurja*), and proof of employment or agriculture to secure the initial handwritten or typed layout of your Relationship and Income Certificates.

### Step 2: Municipal Legalization
The raw certificates from the Ward office are not yet ready for the central government. You must take them to your main **Municipality Office (*Nagarpalika / Gaupalika*)**. A **Section Officer (*Adhikrit*)** must verify the ward signatures, digitize or formally stamp the documents, and apply the official municipal seal.

### Step 3: Nepal MoFA Attestation (Tripureshwor)
With the municipal section officer's stamp secured, you must present the original certificates to the Consular Services Department under the **Ministry of Foreign Affairs (MoFA) in Tripureshwor, Kathmandu**. MoFA evaluates the local government seals and applies the baseline government attestation sticker.

### Step 4: Authorized Italian Translation
Italy maintains an unyielding stance on language compliance. Standard English variations are frequently turned away by financial evaluators. Once your papers clear MoFA, they must be translated into **Italian** by a certified, authorized translator. The translator's stamp and license details must match the standards recognized by the embassy network.

### Step 5: The Italian Diplomatic Network (Kolkata/New Delhi)
The final step requires navigating the Italian consular framework. Depending on your school's location and specific processing paths, your files must be presented either to the **Embassy of Italy in New Delhi** or the **Consulate General of Italy in Kolkata** to receive the final international legalization stamps. 

*(Note: Prior to certain final reviews, verification verification records may be synchronized through the Embassy of Nepal in New Delhi or the Embassy of Nepal in Rome, Italy, to ensure complete state compliance).*
