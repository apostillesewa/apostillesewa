## Introduction

Once your documents are apostilled, attested, or ready for embassy submission, they still need to travel — often internationally, and often on a tight visa or admission deadline. Handing sensitive documents like education certificates, police clearance reports, or apostille packets to a general courier that also ships parcels, electronics, and everyday packages is a common but risky choice. Documents need different handling: careful packaging, priority tracking, and zero tolerance for delay or loss.

## Why Document Courier Needs Are Different

- **Higher stakes, lower tolerance for error.** A misplaced or delayed document package can set back an entire visa or admission timeline by weeks.
- **Careful packaging matters.** Certificates and legal papers require tamper-proof, damage-resistant packaging — not the same treatment as general cargo.
- **Destination sensitivity.** Documents often go to embassies, ministries, or institutions with strict receiving requirements and deadlines.
