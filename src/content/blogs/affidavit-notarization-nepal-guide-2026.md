An **affidavit** (or sworn statement) is a formal written statement of facts declared under oath in front of an authorized official. When applying for foreign visas, university admissions, court proceedings, or family reunification, international authorities require an affidavit to hold legal weight—meaning making a false claim carries legal liability for perjury.

Under **Section 19 of the Notary Public Act 2063 (2006)** and **Rule 16 of the Notary Public Rules 2063**, a licensed Notary Public in Nepal is legally empowered to administer oaths, witness signatures, and notarize affidavits.

This guide explains how affidavit notarization works in Nepal, common types of affidavits required by foreign embassies, statutory fee rules, and how to get your affidavit drafted and notarized online.

---

## What Makes a Notarized Affidavit Legally Valid?

Unlike a simple declaration or support letter, an affidavit is enforceable in court and verified in the official government notary register under **Section 23 of the Notary Public Act**.

+-------------------------------------------------------------------------+
|                    THE AFFIDAVIT NOTARIZATION PROCESS                   |
+-------------------------------------------------------------------------+
| 1. FACT DRAFTING    ➔ Specific, verifiable facts written & formatted.  |
| 2. IDENTITY CHECK   ➔ Original Passport / Citizenship verified.         |
| 3. OATH CEREMONY    ➔ Deponent swears/affirms the facts are true.       |
| 4. WITNESSED SIGN   ➔ Signed directly in front of the Notary Public.   |
| 5. SEAL & REGISTER  ➔ Rule 16 Notary Seal & Sec. 23 Register Entry.     |
+-------------------------------------------------------------------------+

### Key Elements Included in Every Official Affidavit:
* **Deponent Details:** Full legal name, citizenship number, passport number, and permanent address matching official government records.
* **Jurats & Oath Clauses:** Precise legal phrasing (e.g., *"Subscribed and sworn before me..."* or *"I solemnly declare..."*).
* **Annexure List:** Direct references to supporting evidence (such as bank statements, salary certificates, or birth records).
* **Official Notarial Record:** Notary stamp, license number, wet signature, and serial register tracking number.

## Differences: Affidavit vs. Sworn Statement vs. Notarized Letter

Selecting the wrong document format is a leading cause of embassy visa rejections:

| Feature | Affidavit / Sworn Statement | Notarized Letter / Consent | Self-Declaration |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Sworn Under Oath?** | **Yes** (Penalty of Perjury) | No | No |
| **Witnessed by Notary?** | **Yes** | Yes (Signature Only) | No |
| **Legal Weight** | **Highest** (Admissible in court) | Moderate | Lowest |
| **Primary Use Case** | Embassies, Visas, Courts | Ward Offices, Internal Records | Informal Corroboration |

> **Crucial Tip:** If an embassy or visa center (such as VFS Global, TLScontact, or BLS) requests a "Sworn Declaration" or "Statutory Declaration," submitting a simple unsworn letter will result in refusal.

Notarization fees in Nepal are governed by **Rule 20 of the Notary Public Rules 2063**, establishing legal fee ceilings for certified acts.

### Total Costs Include:
1. **Statutory Notarial Fee:** Fixed fee cap under Rule 20 for administering the oath and applying the official seal.

## Avoiding Common Drafting Errors

* **Vague Financial Claims:** Stating *"I have sufficient income"* will be rejected. The affidavit must state specific figures: *"My annual net income for FY 2081/82, as shown in the Tax Clearance Certificate at Annexure A, is NPR X,XXX,XXX."*
* **Unmatched Passport Details:** Ensure all names, dates of birth, and document numbers match your passport exactly.
* **Missing Annexures:** Failing to explicitly list attached supporting documents (bank letters, citizenship copies, ward certificates) inside the body of the affidavit.

## Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)

### What is the difference between an affidavit and a notarized letter?
An affidavit is sworn under oath with criminal liability for false statements (perjury), making it admissible in court and accepted by embassies. A notarized letter only verifies the identity of the person signing it without administering an oath.

### Does the notary need to witness me signing the affidavit?
Yes. Under Section 19 of the Notary Public Act 2063, the signature must be executed in the presence of the Notary Public (or via approved secure video confirmation for remote processing).

### How long is a notarized single-status affidavit valid?
Most foreign embassies and marriage registries require single-status or unmarried affidavits to be **less than 3 to 6 months old** at the time of submission.

### Can an affidavit be used in court in Nepal?
Yes. An affidavit notarized under Section 19 and entered into the Section 23 register book carries evidentiary standing in district, high, and supreme court matters in Nepal.
