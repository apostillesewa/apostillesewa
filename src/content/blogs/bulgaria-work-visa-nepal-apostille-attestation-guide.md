## Introduction

Bulgaria has become an increasingly popular destination for Nepali skilled workers — drivers, technicians, hospitality staff, and operators — thanks to strong demand and a straightforward employer-sponsored pathway. But before you can start working, your Nepali documents must survive a strict multi-step legalization chain and a formal visa process. Getting even one step wrong — a missing MoFA stamp, an untranslated certificate, or the wrong order of submission — can mean rejection at the embassy stage in New Delhi.

This guide walks through exactly what the Bulgarian Type D work visa requires, based on current 2026 rules, so you know what to prepare before you fly to Delhi.

## Key Requirements / What Changed

Bulgaria joined the Schengen Area's land and sea borders in 2025. That means once your Type D visa and residence process is complete, you gain free movement within the Schengen zone for short stays of up to 90 days in any 180-day period — a meaningful upgrade for workers who may need to travel across Europe.

For employment specifically, here's what's required:

- **A confirmed job offer** from a Bulgarian employer — this comes before anything else
- **A work permit** issued by Bulgaria's Ministry of Labour and Social Policy, arranged through your employer
- **Type D (long-stay) visa application**, filed in person at the Bulgarian Embassy in New Delhi, since Bulgaria does not maintain a mission in Nepal
- **Proof of funds**: bank statements showing at least 6 times Bulgaria's minimum monthly wage (currently around €3,721)
- **Private medical insurance**: minimum €30,000 coverage, valid for Bulgaria and ideally the wider Schengen area
- **Certified translations**: any document not already in Bulgarian must carry a certified translation — improperly certified translations are a common cause of rejection
- **Visa fee**: €100 for a standard Type D visa, €200 if applying for the 360-day version
- **In-person interview**: required as part of the application

## Step-by-Step Process

1. **Secure a job offer** from a licensed Bulgarian employer.
2. **Employer applies for your work permit** with the Ministry of Labour and Social Policy in Bulgaria.
3. **Legalize your Police Clearance Certificate (PCC)**: Nepal Police ePCC portal → Ministry of Foreign Affairs (Tripureshwor) → Bulgarian Embassy, New Delhi.
4. **Legalize your education certificate (if required for your role)**: SEE Controller Office → Ministry of Education → MoFA Nepal → Embassy of Nepal, Delhi → Bulgarian Embassy, Delhi.
5. **Assemble supporting documents**: passport, photos, insurance, financial proof, accommodation proof, and certified translations.
6. **Book and attend your Type D visa appointment** at the Bulgarian Embassy in New Delhi — apply at least 3 months before your intended travel date, since processing typically takes 1–2 months.
7. **Attend the in-person interview** and submit your complete file.
8. **Travel to Bulgaria** once your visa is issued, and complete any remaining residence formalities on arrival.
