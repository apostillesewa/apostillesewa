## The Rising Demand for Romania: Work Permits and Study Visas

Romania has rapidly transformed into one of the most popular destinations in Europe for Nepalese citizens. Whether you are a student looking to enroll in a European university or a skilled professional heading into Romania’s booming hospitality, construction, logistics, or agricultural sectors, securing your visa is the final gateway to your new journey.

However, because Romania is a member state of the European Union and a signatory to the Hague Apostille Convention, their immigration authorities maintain strict, uncompromising standards when it comes to verifying foreign paperwork. 

If your documents are issued in Nepal, they must undergo a specific legalization chain before the Romanian government will grant your entry visa.

---

## The Roadblock: No Romanian Embassy in Kathmandu

The single biggest challenge for Nepalese applicants is logistical. **The Republic of Romania does not have an active embassy or consular office handling document legalizations inside Nepal.** 

Instead, the diplomatic mission responsible for processing visas and validating documents for Nepalese citizens is the **Embassy of Romania in New Delhi, India**.

This means that simply getting your documents stamped by local departments in Kathmandu is not enough. Your paperwork must physically travel to Chanakyapuri, New Delhi, to receive the final, crucial consular stamp from the Romanian embassy counter.

---

## The Step-by-Step Document Authentication Chain

To ensure your birth certificates, marriage certificates, police clearances (PCC), or academic transcripts (+2/Bachelor's certificates) are accepted by the Romanian Embassy in Delhi, they must follow this precise order:

### 1. Verification at the Source (Line Ministries)
Before any central government office will look at your paperwork, it must be verified by the department that issued it:
*   **Academic Documents:** Must be verified by the National Examinations Board (NEB) or the university's controller of examinations (e.g., TU, KU), followed by authentication from the Department of Education.
*   **Personal Documents (PCC/Birth/Marriage):** Your Police Clearance Certificate must be issued via the online ePCC portal, and vital certificates must be verified by the local municipality or the Department of Civil Registration.

### 2. Ministry of Foreign Affairs (MoFA) Attestation
Once the source departments verify your certificates, they must be submitted to the Consular Services Department under the Ministry of Foreign Affairs (MoFA) in Tripureshwor, Kathmandu. MoFA checks the previous signatures and applies an official government attestation sticker.

### 3. Romanian Embassy Legalization (New Delhi)
The final step requires taking those MoFA-attested documents directly to the Romanian Embassy in New Delhi. The embassy verifies Nepal's MoFA sticker and applies their own consular stamp, legally preparing your documents for Romanian immigration.

---

## Avoid Common Pitfalls: Spelling and Name Mismatches

Because Romania cross-references your documents across multiple EU security databases, they are highly sensitive to errors. Even a single letter mismatch between your passport name and your academic certificates or PCC can result in an immediate rejection at the Delhi counter. 

Always conduct a thorough audit of your documents to ensure names, dates of birth, and certificate numbers perfectly align before sending them across the border.
