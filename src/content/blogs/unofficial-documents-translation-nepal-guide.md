## What Counts as an "Unofficial" Document Translation?

An "unofficial" translation simply means that the receiving party or destination does not require a legal seal, sworn affidavit, or notary verification.

## Comparison: Unofficial vs. Notarized Translation

Selecting the right translation type prevents rejected submissions at government offices or unnecessary spending on informal texts:

| Feature | Unofficial Translation | Notarized Translation |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Notary Public Seal?** | **No** | **Yes** |
| **Sworn Affidavit Attached?** | **No** | **Yes** (For non-English pairs) |
| **Turnaround Speed** | **Fastest** (Same-Day options) | 1–2 Business Days |
| **Output Formats** | **Editable Word (.docx) / PDF** | Stamped PDF + Wet-Ink Hard Copy |
| **Accepted by Embassies/Courts?** | ❌ **No** | ✅ **Yes** |

> **Crucial Warning:** If your translation is intended for a foreign embassy (e.g., visa applications), university registrar, District Court, bank, or the Ministry of Foreign Affairs (MOFA), you **must use a notarized translation** rather than an unofficial service.

## Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)

### What is the difference between unofficial and notarized translation?
An unofficial translation is executed by a professional translator without an added Notary Public seal or sworn affidavit. A notarized translation carries a formal Notary seal, license number, and entry in the notary register, making it legal proof for embassies, courts, and government authorities.

### Can I submit an unofficial translation to an embassy or visa center?
No. Visa centers, foreign embassies, universities, and government bodies require official notarized translations. Using an unofficial translation for official filings will result in rejection.

### Can I get my translation delivered in Microsoft Word format?
Yes. Unlike notarized translations (which are delivered as physical wet-ink copies or secured PDFs), unofficial translations can be delivered in editable Microsoft Word (`.docx`) or layout-matched PDF formats.

### What happens if I realize later that I need my document notarized?
If your use case changes, you can request an upgrade to a notarized translation. We can add the necessary notarial verification step and issue a stamped, certified copy without having to restart the translation from scratch.
