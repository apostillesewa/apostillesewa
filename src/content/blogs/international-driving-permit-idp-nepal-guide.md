## Introduction

If you are traveling abroad from Nepal for foreign employment, higher studies, tourism, or business, renting a car or legally driving on foreign roads often requires an **International Driving Permit (IDP)**. 

An International Driving Permit is an official booklet that translates your national driving license into multiple languages (such as English, French, Spanish, Russian, and Arabic) in accordance with UN Road Traffic Conventions. It allows foreign traffic police, car rental agencies, and insurance companies to read your credentials.

**Crucial Note:** An IDP is **not** a standalone driving license. It is an official supplement to your original home country smart-card license. You must always carry both your valid Nepali Driving License and your IDP together when driving overseas.

---

## Eligibility & Required Documents

To apply for an IDP in Nepal, you must meet basic prerequisites and present valid paperwork:

### 📋 Key Eligibility Criteria
1. **Age:** Must be at least 18 years old.
2. **Valid Smart Card License:** You must hold a valid, unexpired Nepali Smart-Card Driving License (A, B, C, etc.) issued by the Department of Transport Management (DoTM). *(Temporary paper slips or expired licenses are not accepted)*.
3. **Valid Passport:** Must hold a valid Nepalese Passport.

### 📂 Document Checklist
- **Original Nepali Smart Card Driving License** + 2 clear photocopies.
- **Original Passport** (Details page) + 2 photocopies.
- **Recent Passport-Size Photographs:** 2 to 4 recent auto-size photos.
- **Valid Foreign Visa Copy:** (If applying directly for specific travel destinations).
- **Duly Filled Application Form.**

## Where to Apply in Nepal & Official Issuing Channels

There are two primary channels through which driving permit verification and international permit booklets are processed in Nepal:

1. **Nepal Automobile Association (NASA):** As Nepal's official member body affiliated with the *Fédération Internationale de l'Automobile (FIA)*, NASA is the primary authorized non-governmental body issuing official IDP booklets based on valid national licenses.
2. **Department of Transport Management (DoTM / Ministry of Physical Infrastructure):** DoTM issues the underlying official smart card license and provides foreign translation attestations or official verification letters required by specific foreign embassies and transport authorities.

---

## Official Fees & Validity

* **Validity Period:** An IDP is typically issued for **1 to 3 years** (or until the expiration date of your original Nepali driving license, whichever comes first).
* **Official Charges:** Government and official club fees generally range from **NPR 3,000 to NPR 10,000+** depending on the selected validity period (1-year vs 3-year), processing speed, and category endorsements.

> **Beware of Online IDP Scams:** Many unofficial websites claim to sell instant downloadable "International Driving Cards" within minutes. These third-party digital cards are frequently **rejected** by foreign traffic police and rental desks. Always obtain an official, physical permit tied to your national license.

## Step-by-Step IDP Application Process


```

[Verify Nepali Smart Card License] ➔ [Prepare Document Copies & Passport] ➔ [Submit Application to NASA / DoTM] ➔ [Fee Payment] ➔ [Biometric Check & Booklet Collection]

```

1. **Verify Your License Status:** Ensure your Nepali smart-card license has at least 6 months of remaining validity and covers the specific vehicle category (e.g., Category A for motorbikes, Category B for cars) you wish to drive abroad.
2. **Document Pre-Verification:** Prepare your original passport, citizenship card, photos, and license copies.
3. **Application Submission:** Submit the physical or online portal application through the authorized processing desk.
4. **Fee Settlement:** Pay the prescribed application fee and retain your official payment receipt.
5. **Booklet Collection:** Collect your stamped IDP booklet, ensuring all printed transliterations, passport details, and vehicle categories match your passport and home license.

## When Do You Need Driving License Verification or Legalization?

In many European, Asian, and Middle Eastern countries (such as Poland, Germany, UAE, Japan, or Portugal), simply holding an IDP is not enough for long-term residency or converting your permit into a local foreign driving license. Foreign road transport authorities frequently require your **original Nepali Driving License to be legally authenticated** before recognizing your driving experience.

This requires:
- **Nepal DoTM / Ministry Verification Letter:** Verification from the Department of Transport Management in Kathmandu confirming the license is genuine.
- **Nepal Ministry of Foreign Affairs (MoFA) Attestation:** Stamping and QR verification at the Department of Consular Services in Kathmandu.
- **Embassy Superlegalization / Legal Translation:** Official sworn translation into the destination country's language (e.g., Polish, German, Spanish) and legalization at the accredited foreign embassy.
