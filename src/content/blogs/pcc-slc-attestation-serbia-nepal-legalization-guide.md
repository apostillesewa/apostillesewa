## Introduction

If you're heading to Serbia for work or study, there's a document hurdle most applicants don't see coming: Serbia has no embassy in Nepal that can legalize your documents. Your Police Clearance Certificate (PCC) and SLC/SEE education certificate both have to make a physical journey to New Delhi — and unlike some destination countries, Serbia's process runs through **two separate Delhi embassy stops**, not one.

Get the order wrong, skip a stop, or submit an expired document, and the Serbian Embassy will reject the package. Here's the exact chain, broken down by document type.

## Key Requirements / What Changed

Serbia is not a party to the Hague Apostille Convention in a way that simplifies this for Nepali nationals, so an apostille alone will not satisfy the requirement. Instead, both your PCC and SLC need full **chain legalization**:

- **Ministry of Foreign Affairs (MoFA), Nepal** — attestation in Tripureshwor, required for both document types
- **Embassy of Nepal, New Delhi** — a consular stamp confirming the document's Nepali origin, required before the Serbian step
- **Embassy of Serbia, New Delhi (Chanakyapuri)** — the final legalization stamp directly on your PCC or SLC

This is the step most people miss: many applicants assume MoFA attestation alone is enough, or that only one Delhi embassy visit is needed. For Serbia, **both** the Embassy of Nepal and the Embassy of Serbia must stamp the document before it's accepted.

## Step-by-Step Process

**For your Police Clearance Certificate (PCC):**
1. Apply through Nepal Police's ePCC portal
2. Get MoFA attestation in Tripureshwor
3. Submit to the Embassy of Nepal, New Delhi for consular stamping
4. Submit to the Embassy of Serbia, New Delhi for final legalization

**For your SLC/SEE Education Certificate:**
1. Verify your marksheet at the SEE Controller Office
2. Get endorsement from the Ministry of Education
3. Get MoFA attestation in Tripureshwor
4. Submit to the Embassy of Nepal, New Delhi for consular stamping
5. Submit to the Embassy of Serbia, New Delhi for final legalization

Both chains converge on the same two Delhi stops, but the SLC path has one extra step at the start (Controller Office → Ministry of Education) before it joins the PCC's route through MoFA.
