When applying for foreign student visas, international travel, commercial bank loans, public tenders, or corporate tax filings in Nepal, demonstrating verified financial standing is a strict requirement. A **CA Net Worth Certificate** or **Audited Financial Statement** provides legal proof of liquid and fixed financial assets.

Issued by a Chartered Accountant registered with the **Institute of Chartered Accountants of Nepal (ICAN)**, these reports synthesize bank deposits, real estate holdings, share portfolios, and income streams alongside verified liability disclosures into a standardized legal format.

This guide details the core use cases, required supporting documents, embassy freshness standards, and how to combine CA certification withNotary Public attestation.

### 1. Overseas Student Visa Applications
Embassies for the USA, UK, Australia, Canada, Schengen, Japan, New Zealand, and South Korea require a **Sponsor Net Worth Certificate** covering the student's parents or legal guardians. It aggregates total family assets against outstanding liabilities to ensure long-term tuition and living cost coverage.

### 2. Tourist & Visitor Visa Financial Proof
Embassy entry reviews for tourist, visitor, and business travel require a **Financial Standing Statement** to verify adequate travel funding and establish strong financial home ties back to Nepal.

### 3. Bank Education Loans
Nepali commercial banks (including Nabil Bank, NIC Asia, Global IME, NMB Bank, and Standard Chartered) require a **Guarantor / Sponsor Net Worth Certificate** before approving education loans to verify collateral capacity.

### 4. Public & Commercial Tender Bids
Government and corporate tender applications demand an **Audited Turnover Certificate** covering the last 3 fiscal years to prove eligibility and operational scale.

### 5. Annual IRD Tax Filings & Corporate Compliance
Companies and PAN/VAT-registered firms must submit annual **Audited Financial Statements** (Balance Sheet, Profit & Loss, and Cash Flow) signed by an ICAN-certified auditor to the Inland Revenue Department (IRD).

## 4 Common Reasons Visa Officers & Banks Reject CA Reports

1. **Unsubstantiated Asset Valuations:** Listing property or shares at arbitrary values without attaching supporting municipal tax assessments, bank balance slips, or stock market statements.
2. **Omission of Outstanding Liabilities:** Omitting existing personal, vehicle, or mortgage loans. Destination authorities cross-reference credit history, and undisclosed debts lead to file rejection for financial misrepresentation.
3. **Missing ICAN Registration Credentials:** Reports lacking an active ICAN membership number, official firm seal, or date stamp are treated as informal opinions and rejected.
4. **Expired Validity Period:** Visa centers strictly enforce a **3-to-6-month freshness rule** for net worth certificates. Submitting a report older than 6 months usually results in a request for updated financial proof.

## Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)

### Is a simple bank statement enough for a student visa financial submission?
No. Embassies require a comprehensive CA Net Worth Certificate that consolidates liquid bank balances, fixed deposits, property assets, share investments, and regular income streams into a single verified report.

### Do I also need a Notary Public stamp on my CA Net Worth Certificate?
Yes. Most foreign visa centers (such as VFS Global, TLScontact, and BLS) require both the Chartered Accountant's ICAN seal and an official Notary Public certification on the report.

### How long does it take to prepare a CA Net Worth Certificate?
For routine student visa applications with complete financial documentation, the CA report takes **2 to 4 business days** to prepare, certify, and notarize.

### Whose net worth should be certified for a student visa?
The report typically covers the student's primary financial sponsors—usually parents, legal guardians, or working spouses who are providing financial support for the study abroad period.
