## Introduction

If you are a student, skilled worker, or professional from Nepal preparing to move to Lithuania, navigating document verification is one of the most critical steps in your visa journey. To obtain your Temporary Residence Permit (TRP) or finalize university enrollment with the Lithuanian Migration Department (**MIGRIS**), you must present a fully legalized Police Clearance Certificate (PCC).

For years, Nepalese applicants relied on the "Denmark Route"—sending documents through the Embassy of Nepal in Copenhagen for concurrent accreditation handling. However, following diplomatic restructuring and the formal closure of the Nepalese Embassy in Copenhagen, **the Denmark route is now completely shut down**. 

If you attempt to follow old online blogs recommending Copenhagen, your documents will be returned unprocessed, risking costly delays or missed MIGRIS application deadlines. Here is the updated, official 2026 document legalization chain for Lithuania.

## Key Requirements / What Changed

The new, official legalization chain for Lithuania-bound applicants is:

**Nepal Police Portal (PCC) → Nepal MOFA Attestation (Kathmandu) → Embassy of Nepal, New Delhi → Embassy of Lithuania / MoFA Vilnius Counter-Legalization**

- **Closure of the Copenhagen Channel:** The Embassy of Nepal in Copenhagen no longer handles consular legalizations for Lithuania or the Nordic region. All concurrent accreditation functions have been redirected.
- **New Delhi Diplomatic Hub:** The **Embassy of Nepal in New Delhi** now acts as the primary diplomatic bridge for cross-verifying Nepalese Ministry of Foreign Affairs (MoFA) stamps before documents enter European consular channels accredited to Lithuania.
- **Strict 3-Month PCC Freshness Window:** MIGRIS strictly enforces a validity window on background checks. Your Police Clearance Certificate must be issued within **3 months** of your physical or digital submission to the Lithuanian migration authorities.
- **Physical QR-Sticker Requirement:** Plain digital printouts of your online police report will be rejected. The PCC must carry a physical QR-coded attestation sticker issued by the Department of Consular Services (Tripureshwor, Kathmandu).

## Step-by-Step Process

1. **Obtain Official Police Clearance Certificate (PCC)** — Apply for an electronic PCC through the official Nepal Police online portal. Ensure all personal details, passport numbers, and spelling match your valid passport exactly.
2. **Nepal MOFA QR-Attestation (Kathmandu)** — Upload your digital PCC to the Department of Consular Services (NEATS) portal in Kathmandu, schedule a physical verification slot, and obtain the physical QR-coded authentication sticker at the Tripureshwor desk.
3. **Embassy of Nepal, New Delhi Cross-Verification** — Dispatch the MoFA-attested PCC to New Delhi, where the Nepalese High Commission cross-verifies the MoFA QR sticker and applies its official diplomatic seal.
4. **Lithuanian Diplomatic Accreditation / MoFA Vilnius Counter-Legalization** — Submit the Embassy-verified PCC to the competent Lithuanian diplomatic mission or Ministry of Foreign Affairs in Vilnius for final superlegalization.
5. **MIGRIS Portal Submission** — Upload clear, high-resolution scans of the fully superlegalized PCC with all back-page stamps to your *MIGRIS* online portal account and present the original during your biometric appointment.

**Estimated timeline:** roughly 12–20 working days end-to-end, depending on courier transit between Kathmandu and New Delhi and embassy counter queues.

## Common Pitfalls & Rejection Risks

- **Following Outdated "Denmark Route" Information:** Sending original documents to Denmark or paying obsolete Copenhagen embassy accounts.
- **Submitting Expired Police Reports:** Allowing the PCC to pass its 90-day validity mark while waiting for diplomatic courier transit or embassy appointments.
- **Relying on Plain Online Printouts:** Attempting to submit a police certificate directly to foreign authorities without the mandatory physical Nepal MoFA sticker and diplomatic cross-verification stamps.
- **Name & Passport Discrepancies:** Minor variations in middle names, family names, or passport numbers across your Nagarikta, passport, and police records.
