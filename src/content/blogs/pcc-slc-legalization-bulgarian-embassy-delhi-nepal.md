## Introduction

If you are a Nepali skilled worker — a driver, operator, mechanic, or technician — preparing to work in Bulgaria, you have probably already realized that getting your documents legalized is not a simple one-stop process. Unlike countries that accept a single Apostille stamp, Bulgaria requires full **consular legalization**, meaning your documents must travel through multiple government offices in Nepal *and* physically reach the **Bulgarian Embassy in Chanakyapuri, New Delhi, India** for a final official stamp.

The most common mistake applicants make? Assuming all documents follow the same legalization path. They don't. Your **Police Clearance Certificate (PCC)** and your **SLC/SEE educational certificate** follow two completely different chains — and missing even one step results in rejection at the embassy counter.

This guide breaks down both chains clearly so you know exactly what to prepare.

---

## Key Requirements: Two Documents, Two Chains

Bulgarian Embassy consular officers require Nepali skilled workers to submit two core documents:

1. **Police Clearance Certificate (PCC)** — proves you have a clean criminal background
2. **SLC / SEE Certificate** — proves your educational qualification and identity as a skilled worker

Each document must go through its own government verification chain before the Bulgarian Embassy will accept it.

---

## Step-by-Step Process

### Chain 1: PCC Legalization (3 Steps)

**Step 1 — Department of Police (Nepal Police ePCC Portal)**
Apply for your Police Clearance Certificate through the official Nepal Police ePCC online portal. This step is **free of charge**. Once processed, you will receive your official PCC document.

**Step 2 — Ministry of Foreign Affairs (MoFA), Tripureshwor**
Bring your original PCC to the MoFA office in Tripureshwor, Kathmandu. MoFA will apply their official attestation sticker, verifying the document as genuine for international use.

**Step 3 — Bulgarian Embassy, Chanakyapuri, New Delhi**
The MoFA-attested PCC must be physically submitted at the Bulgarian Embassy counter in New Delhi for the final consular legalization stamp. This is the step that gives your document full legal standing in Bulgaria.

> **Summary:** Department of Police → MoFA Nepal → Bulgarian Embassy New Delhi

---

### Chain 2: SLC / SEE Educational Certificate Legalization (5 Steps)

**Step 1 — SEE Controller Office (Examination Controller Office)**
Your original SLC or SEE marksheet must first be verified at the Examination Controller Office. This is the primary source verification confirming the authenticity of your academic record.

**Step 2 — Ministry of Education (MoE)**
The Controller-verified document then goes to the Ministry of Education for its official endorsement, confirming it as a legitimate Nepali educational credential.

**Step 3 — Ministry of Foreign Affairs (MoFA), Tripureshwor**
MoFA applies its attestation sticker, clearing the document for international submission.

**Step 4 — Embassy of Nepal in New Delhi**
Before the Bulgarian Embassy will accept it, the document must first be presented at the **Embassy of Nepal in New Delhi** for an additional verification layer. This is a mandatory step unique to educational documents and is often missed by applicants handling this independently.

**Step 5 — Bulgarian Embassy, Chanakyapuri, New Delhi**
Only after completing all four prior steps can your SLC/SEE be submitted at the Bulgarian Embassy for final consular legalization.

> **Summary:** SEE Controller Office → Ministry of Education → MoFA Nepal → Embassy of Nepal Delhi → Bulgarian Embassy Delhi

---

## Common Mistakes That Lead to Rejection

- Submitting PCC without MoFA attestation
- Skipping the **Embassy of Nepal in Delhi** step for the SLC/SEE (the most frequently missed step)
- Submitting expired documents
- Incorrect document order in the submission package
- Traveling to Delhi without a prior document audit
