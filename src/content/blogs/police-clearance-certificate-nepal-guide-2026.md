A **Police Clearance Certificate (PCC)**—commonly known across Nepal as a *Police Report*, *Police Character Certificate*, or *Background Check Certificate*—is an official legal record issued by the **Nepal Police Headquarters (Character Verification Section)** under the Ministry of Home Affairs. 

Whether you are preparing for a foreign study visa, overseas employment, permanent residency (PR), or marriage abroad, submitting an accurate, unexpired PCC is mandatory. 

Thanks to the digitized **OPCR (Online Police Clearance Report)** portal and the government's **Nagarik App**, obtaining a Police Clearance Certificate in Nepal is **completely free of cost** and can be done entirely online without visiting a police station.

This comprehensive 2026 guide covers the exact OPCR online application steps, document upload standards, the mandatory digital MOFA attestation workflow, fee breakdowns, and common rejection triggers.

## 2. Who Requires a Nepal Police Clearance Certificate?

Foreign embassies, immigration authorities, and international employers require a verified Nepal PCC for:
* **Student Visas:** Mandatory for universities and embassies in Australia, the UK, USA, Canada, Japan, South Korea, and Schengen nations.
* **Foreign Employment & Z/Work Visas:** Required by labor ministries in Gulf Cooperation Council (GCC) countries, Malaysia, Europe, and Asia before issuing work permits.
* **Permanent Residency (PR) & Immigration:** Essential for Express Entry (Canada), PR applications (Australia/NZ), and green card processing.
* **Family Reunification & Marriage Abroad:** Required when registering foreign marriages or joining a spouse overseas.
* **Non-Resident Nepali (NRN) Cards:** Necessary background verification for NRN card applications.

## 3. Mandatory Documents Checklist

Before initiating your online application on the OPCR portal or Nagarik App, scan original copies of all required documents. Uploading blurry scans or smartphone photos taken at bad angles is the leading cause of rejection.

| Required Document | Technical Specification / Formatting Rule |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Nepali Citizenship Card** | Color scan of **both Front & Back** (Details must match passport identically). |
| **Valid Nepali Passport** | Color scan of the **Bio-data Page (Page 2/3)** and **Page 31 (Address Page)**. |
| **Passport-Sized Photograph** | Recent digital photo (White background, neutral expression, clear ears). |
| **Marriage Certificate** *(If Applicable)* | Required if your surname changed post-marriage or if spouse details are listed. |
| **Proof of Foreign Travel / Purpose** | Visa copy, university offer letter, job contract, or travel itinerary. |
| **NOC / Ward Recommendation** *(If requested)* | Ward office address verification for specific local address updates. |

## 4. How to Apply Online (Method 1: OPCR Portal - Recommended)

The web-based **Online Police Clearance Registration (OPCR)** portal (`opcr.nepalpolice.gov.np`) is the primary system for all Nepali citizens and foreign residents.

+-------------------------------------------------------------------------+
|                       OPCR APPLICATION WORKFLOW                         |
+-------------------------------------------------------------------------+
| 1. ACCOUNT CREATION ➔ Register using a active email & mobile number.    |
| 2. DATA ENTRY       ➔ Fill personal, passport, & permanent ward details.|
| 3. FILE UPLOADS     ➔ Upload high-res PDF/JPEG scans (Max 1 MB/file).   |
| 4. SUBMISSION       ➔ Obtain your tracking Reference ID (NPR 0 fee).    |
| 5. DIGITAL DOWNLOAD ➔ Download official QR-coded PDF within 2–3 days.  |
+-------------------------------------------------------------------------+


### Step-by-Step Instructions:
1. **Portal Registration:** Go to [opcr.nepalpolice.gov.np](https://opcr.nepalpolice.gov.np/) and click **"Register"**. Use an email address you access daily (system notifications and download links are sent here).
2. **Profile Creation:** Enter your full legal name exactly as printed on your Citizenship Card.
3. **Application Form Filing:** Select your permanent district and Ward number. Input your passport number, issue date, and expiry date accurately.
4. **Document Upload:** Upload clear JPEG or PDF files (under 1 MB each). Ensure text on your citizenship card is 100% legible.
5. **Application Submission:** Review all entries and submit. Save your **Application Reference Number** for online status tracking.
6. **Download Certificate:** Once your status changes to "Approved" (typically within 48 to 72 hours), log in to download your digitally signed PCC with an embedded QR code.

## 5. Mobile Application Process (Method 2: Nagarik App)

If you prefer completing your application entirely via smartphone, use the official Government of Nepal **Nagarik App**:

1. **Install App:** Download the **Nagarik App** from Google Play Store or Apple App Store (ensure the publisher is *Government of Nepal*).
2. **Identity Verification:** Log in with your SIM card (registered under your citizenship number) and complete biometric/citizenship verification.
3. **Navigate to Police Clearance:** Tap **Services ➔ Nepal Police ➔ Police Clearance Certificate**.
4. **Data Sync & Upload:** The app automatically populates your citizenship details. Upload your passport scan and photograph directly via your camera or file manager.
5. **Submit & Download:** Track application status within the app and download the approved QR-coded PDF directly to your device.

## 6. Offline Application (District Police Office)

For citizens unable to access digital tools or who require offline verification:
* **Where to Go:** Visit your respective **District Police Office (*Jilla Prahari Karyalaya*)** or the **Metropolitan Police Headquarters (Naxal, Kathmandu)**.
* **Government Fee:** NPR 500 bank voucher payment.
* **Processing Time:** 1 to 3 weeks (significantly slower than online digital processing).

## 7. Applying from Overseas (NRNs & Nepali Expats)

If you are currently residing abroad and need a fresh Nepal PCC:
1. **Online Self-Application:** You can complete the OPCR process online from anywhere in the world, provided you upload clear scans of your passport, citizenship card, and current foreign visa stamp.
2. **Embassy Channel:** Alternatively, submit your request through the nearest Nepali Diplomatic Mission/Embassy (processing takes 3 to 6 weeks).
3. **Authorized Representative:** Authorize a family member or agency in Nepal to manage local attestation chains on your behalf.

## 9. Common Rejection Reasons & How to Avoid Them

| Rejection Cause | Why It Fails | How to Prevent It |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Name Mismatch** | Passport spelling differs from Citizenship Card. | Obtain a notarized **One-and-the-Same Affidavit** or update municipal records first. |
| **Blurry Uploads** | OPCR automated tools reject unreadable text or low-resolution scans. | Scan documents at minimum **300 DPI** in bright, even lighting. |
| **Expired Passport** | Submitting a passport with less than 6 months validity. | Renew your passport prior to starting your OPCR application. |
| **Incorrect Ward Details** | Permanent address listed does not match your citizenship certificate. | Double-check local administrative ward numbers before submitting. |
| **Unresolved Court Cases** | Active criminal proceedings or pending court summons. | PCC will be withheld until an official court clearance verdict is presented. |

---

## 10. PCC Expiration & Document Freshness Rules

Nepal Police does not print an explicit expiration date on the PCC document. Validity is determined strictly by the receiving embassy or foreign authority:

* **Schengen Visas (Europe):** Must be issued within **3 months** of your visa appointment.
* **UK, USA, Australia, & Canada:** Typically accepts certificates issued within **6 months**.
* **Foreign Employment (GCC / Malaysia):** Must be issued within **3 months** of work contract clearance.

> **Pro-Tip:** Do not apply for your PCC too far in advance. Obtain it 2 to 4 weeks before your scheduled visa appointment to ensure it remains valid throughout processing.

## Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)

### What is the government fee for an online Police Clearance Certificate in Nepal?
Applying online via the OPCR portal (`opcr.nepalpolice.gov.np`) or the Nagarik App is **100% free of charge**. Government fees (NPR 500) apply only for physical offline applications at District Police Offices or for subsequent MOFA attestation (NPR 500).

### How long does it take to download an online PCC in Nepal?
Under normal conditions, online OPCR applications are processed within **2 to 3 working days**. Digital certificates are downloaded directly from the portal.

### Do I need to physically visit Nepal Police Headquarters in Naxal?
No. For online applications, physical visits to Naxal or District Police Offices are no longer required for Nepali citizens. The entire process—from filing to certificate collection—is completed digitally.

### Can a foreign national who lived in Nepal get a Police Clearance Certificate?
Yes. Foreign nationals can apply on the OPCR portal by selecting the "Foreigner" category, or by submitting an offline request at the Metropolitan Police Office in Kathmandu. A valid passport, Nepal visa stamps, and proof of residence are required.
