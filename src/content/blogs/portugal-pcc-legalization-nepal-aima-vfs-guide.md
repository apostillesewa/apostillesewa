## Introduction

If you are navigating documentation for Portugal, one file frequently creates confusion and unexpected delays: your Police Clearance Certificate (PCC). A common misconception among Nepalese applicants is that everyone applying for a Portugal visa needs a fully legalized PCC certified by the Portuguese Embassy in New Delhi.

In reality, consular rules strictly differentiate between applicants applying from within Nepal and those already living in Portugal. Knowing where you stand before submitting documents is critical to saving time, money, and avoiding unnecessary rejections.

---

## The Critical Jurisdiction Rule: Who Needs Legalization?

Before starting the authentication process, you must verify if your application category requires diplomatic legalization:

* **Applicants Applying from Nepal (Work / Study / D1 Visas):** The Embassy of Portugal in New Delhi **does NOT accept or perform** PCC document legalization for applicants who are still residing in Nepal applying for initial entry visas. Initial visa applicants submit their standard Nepal MoFA-attested documents directly with their visa file as instructed by VFS/Consular guidelines.
* **Applicants Already in Portugal (AIMA / Status Regularization / Renewals):** If you are already residing in Lisbon, Porto, or elsewhere in Portugal and need to submit or extend your Police Clearance Certificate for **AIMA (Agência para a Integração, Migrações e Asilo)** status regularization, **full embassy legalization is mandatory.**

---

## The 3-Step Legalization Chain (For AIMA / Applicants in Portugal)

For those residing in Portugal who require a fully superlegalized PCC, your document must pass through three distinct government and diplomatic stages across Nepal and India:

### Step 1: Nepal MoFA Attestation (Kathmandu)
Your original Police Clearance Certificate issued by Nepal Police must first be verified, stamped, and authenticated by the Department of Consular Services at the Ministry of Foreign Affairs (MoFA) in Kathmandu.

### Step 2: Embassy of Nepal Counter-Legalization (New Delhi)
Once MoFA Kathmandu has applied its physical attestation, the original document must travel to India. The Embassy of Nepal in New Delhi cross-verifies the MoFA stamp and applies its official diplomatic seal.

### Step 3: VFS Delhi Portugal Application Center
Only after both Nepalese authorities have completed their attestations can the document be physically submitted to the VFS Delhi Portugal Application Center for final consular legalization by Portuguese diplomatic officers.

---

## Key Rules You Can't Miss

* **Original Documents Only:** Unverified digital printouts, plain scans, or photocopies are rejected without review.
* **Strict 90-Day Validity Window:** Portuguese authorities enforce a 90-day expiration mark on Police Clearance Certificates from the date of issuance. An expired PCC will be rejected at the VFS counter.
* **Power of Attorney (POA) Authorized Submissions:** Applicants living in Portugal do not need to fly back to Asia. Third-party representatives can execute the complete process on your behalf using a signed Power of Attorney letter alongside copy ID verification.
* **Exemption for Minors:** Applicants under 16 years of age are not required to present a police clearance record.
* **Strict Submission Timings:** VFS Global / Portugal Consular submission counters in New Delhi operate within strict morning slots (9:00 AM – 12:00 PM).
