## Introduction

Pursuing a foreign degree program right here in Nepal—such as studying at local colleges affiliated with Malaysian universities (e.g., Asia Pacific University / APIIT Malaysia)—is a popular pathway for Nepalese students. However, if you plan to use your Malaysian Bachelor’s or Master’s degree to apply for higher studies, a Master's program, or a post-study visa in European countries like **Austria, Italy, Germany, or Malta**, you will face a unique diplomatic challenge.

Because your academic degree is technically **issued under the Malaysian higher education authority**, European universities and foreign embassies (such as the Embassy of Austria or Embassy of Italy) will **not** accept your Malaysian degree with only local Nepal notary stamps. Because your degree originates from a non-Hague/foreign framework, it must go through a dual-country cross-verification chain across **Malaysia, Nepal, and the target European embassy**.

Here is the complete step-by-step guide to legalizing your Malaysian degree from Nepal for European university admissions and visa processing.

## The Legalization Challenge: Why a Dual-Country Chain Is Required

When a Nepali student earns a Malaysian university degree from a college in Kathmandu:
1. **The Awarding University is in Malaysia:** The academic transcript and degree certificate are officially registered under the Malaysian Ministry of Higher Education (MOHE).
2. **The Student is Living in Nepal:** You need to present this qualification to the Ministry of Education (MOE) in Nepal, the Ministry of Foreign Affairs (MoFA) in Kathmandu, and foreign embassies (e.g., Embassy of Austria in New Delhi or Embassy of Italy in Kathmandu/Delhi) to obtain your visa or **Declaration of Value (DOV) / Equivalence**.

To bridge these jurisdiction boundaries, your degree must follow a strict multi-tier legalization chain.

## Step-by-Step Legalization Chain


```

[Malaysia MOHE Verification] ➔ [Wisma Putra (MOFA Malaysia)] ➔ [Embassy of Nepal, Kuala Lumpur / High Commission of Malaysia] ➔ [Nepal MOE & Equivalence] ➔ [Nepal MOFA QR-Attestation] ➔ [Destination European Embassy (Austria / Italy)]

```

### Step 1: Verification by Ministry of Higher Education (MOHE) Malaysia
Before your degree can leave Malaysia's education registry, the original degree certificate and transcripts must be submitted to the **Ministry of Higher Education (MOHE / KPM)** in Putrajaya, Malaysia. MOHE verifies that the awarding university and your student transcript are genuine and applies an official authentication stamp.

### Step 2: MOFA Malaysia (Wisma Putra) Attestation
Once MOHE approves the documents, they must be submitted to the **Consular Division of the Ministry of Foreign Affairs, Malaysia (Wisma Putra)** in Putrajaya. Wisma Putra verifies the MOHE officer’s signature and applies the official Malaysian diplomatic legalization stamp.

### Step 3: Embassy Cross-Verification (Kuala Lumpur / New Delhi)
To bridge the Malaysian government authentication with Nepal’s official records, the Wisma Putra-stamped originals must be counter-verified by the **Embassy of Nepal in Kuala Lumpur, Malaysia** (or the High Commission of Malaysia accredited to the region).

### Step 4: Nepal Ministry of Education (MOE) & Equivalence Approval
With your degree certified by the Malaysian authorities and diplomatic channels, bring the documents back to Kathmandu:
- **MOE Verification:** Submit your transcripts and certificates to the Ministry of Education, Science and Technology (Sanothimi, Bhaktapur) for local academic verification.
- **TU Equivalence Certificate (If Required):** If applying for public university admissions or civil services in Europe, obtain an official Academic Equivalence Certificate from **Tribhuvan University (TU) Curriculum Development Centre**.

### Step 5: Nepal MOFA QR-Attestation (Kathmandu)
Upload your verified documents to the **Department of Consular Services (NEATS portal)** in Tripureshwor, Kathmandu. Once verified, attend your physical slot to receive the official QR-coded Nepal MoFA authentication sticker.

### Step 6: Final Legalization at the Destination European Embassy
Now your document is ready for your destination country:
- **For Austria:** Submit the MoFA-attested file to the Embassy of Austria (or its accredited consular desk in New Delhi) for superlegalization and certified translation into German.
- **For Italy:** Submit your degree chain to the Embassy of Italy for the **Dichiarazione di Valore (Declaration of Value - DOV)** or CIMEA verification required for university enrollment and study visas.
- **For Germany / Malta / Other EU States:** Submit to the respective embassy for final diplomatic legalization or uni-assist verification.

## Common Pitfalls & Rejection Risks

- **Skipping MOHE Malaysia:** Sending documents directly to Wisma Putra without prior MOHE approval will lead to immediate rejection in Malaysia.
- **Relying on Private Notary Stamps:** European embassies (Austria, Italy, Germany) strictly refuse unverified local notary stamps on foreign degrees.
- **Name & Passport Discrepancies:** Ensure your Romanized name spelling on your Malaysian degree transcript matches your Nepalese Passport and Citizenship exactly.
