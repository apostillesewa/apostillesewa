## Introduction

Heading abroad for foreign employment is a life-changing milestone for thousands of Nepalis. But before packing your bags and heading to Tribhuvan International Airport (TIA), there's one document you legally cannot leave without: **Shram Swikriti** (श्रम स्वीकृति), the official Labor Approval Certificate.

Thanks to the Department of Foreign Employment (DoFE), you no longer need to stand in long queues at Tahachal — the entire application process is now handled digitally through the **Foreign Employment Information Management System (FEIMS)** portal.

This guide breaks down what Shram Swikriti is, the documents you'll need, and a complete step-by-step walkthrough of getting your e-approval online.

## What Is Shram Swikriti and Why Is It Mandatory?

Shram Swikriti is an official authorization issued under the Ministry of Labor, Employment, and Social Security. It confirms that:

* Your foreign employment contract is legitimate
* You're covered by mandatory life insurance
* You've contributed to the Foreign Employment Welfare Fund, which supports rescue and emergency services for workers abroad

**Important:** Departing Nepal for foreign work without Shram Swikriti is illegal. Airport immigration will deny entry if your passport isn't linked to an active digital approval.

## Documents You'll Need

Before starting your application, have clear scans (PDF or JPG) of:

1. **Passport** — minimum 6 months of remaining validity
2. **Work Visa / Entry Permit** — issued by the destination country
3. **Employment Offer Letter / Contract** — stamped or authenticated where required
4. **Medical Fitness Certificate** — from a DoFE-approved medical institution
5. **Pre-Departure Orientation Certificate** — required for first-time individual applicants
6. **Bank Account Details** — a local Nepali account for salary and welfare fund integration

## Step-by-Step Process on FEIMS

1. **Register / Login on FEIMS** — Visit feims.dofe.gov.np, select Individual Login, and set up your profile with your passport and mobile number.
2. **Choose Your Approval Category** — New Individual Approval (first-time applicants) or Re-entry/Renewal (returning to the same employer).
3. **Fill Personal & Employment Details** — Enter your address, emergency contact, destination country, employer, job position, and salary.
4. **Upload Scanned Documents** — Submit your passport, visa, contract, medical certificate, and orientation certificate under their designated slots.
5. **Pay Insurance & Welfare Fees Online** — Complete payment through digital wallets like eSewa, Khalti, or ConnectIPS. *(Confirm current fee amounts directly on the FEIMS portal, as these are periodically revised.)*
6. **Track Status & Download Certificate** — Save your reference token number, then log back in once approved to download and print your official e-certificate.

### A Few Tips

* **Double-check passport details** — a small spelling mismatch between your application and your passport can cause instant rejection.
* **No physical stamping required** — DoFE no longer stamps passports. The digital PDF with a QR code is what matters; print at least 2 copies to carry with your travel documents.
* **Apply early** — processing can take a few working days, and peak seasons may see delays, so don't leave this to the last minute.
