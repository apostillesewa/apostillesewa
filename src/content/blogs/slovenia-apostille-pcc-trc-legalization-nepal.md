## Introduction
Slovenia has become an increasingly popular Schengen destination for Nepali students and skilled workers — but Slovenia has no embassy in Nepal. Every document, and every approved permit, must go through the Embassy of Slovenia in New Delhi. Students, workers, and those who've already been approved for a Temporary Residence Card (TRC) each face a different process — and getting the wrong one wrong can mean delays.

**⚠️ Note: The steps below reflect our current understanding of the process and need confirmation against the Nepal Ministry of Foreign Affairs (MoFA) and the Embassy of Slovenia, New Delhi, before this is treated as official guidance.**

## Key Requirements / What Changed
- **Students** need their educational certificates (SLC/SEE, +2, degree, etc.) legalized before a study visa can be issued.
- **Workers** need a legalized Police Clearance Certificate (PCC) as part of their work permit application.
- **TRC holders** — applicants already approved for residence — often need their physical card collected from the embassy, which normally requires a personal trip to Delhi.

## Step-by-Step Process

### For Workers: PCC Legalization (3 Steps)
1. **Department of Police** – Apply via the Nepal Police ePCC portal.
2. **Ministry of Foreign Affairs (MoFA)** – Attestation sticker, Tripureshwor, Kathmandu.
3. **Embassy of Slovenia, New Delhi** – Final consular legalization.

### For Students: Educational Certificate Legalization (5 Steps)
1. **Controller / Board Office** – Source verification of the certificate.
2. **Ministry of Education** – Official academic endorsement.
3. **Ministry of Foreign Affairs (MoFA)** – Attestation sticker, Tripureshwor.
4. **Embassy of Nepal, New Delhi** – Counter-legalization *(to be confirmed as a Slovenia requirement)*.
5. **Embassy of Slovenia, New Delhi** – Final consular legalization.

### For TRC Holders: Document Collection Service
If your Temporary Residence Card has been approved, it often needs to be physically collected from the Embassy of Slovenia in Delhi. Instead of applicants flying to India, we can collect the card on your behalf under a Power of Attorney or equivalent embassy-recognized authorization.
