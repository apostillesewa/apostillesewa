## Introduction

If you are a Nepali national living in Portugal and preparing for your AIMA (Agência para a Integração, Migrações e Asilo) appointment, obtaining and legalizing your documents from Nepal is a critical step. Whether you are applying for your initial Temporary Residence Permit (TRC), renewing your status, or bringing family members under family reunification, AIMA strictly enforces document authenticity guidelines.

Because Nepal is not part of the Hague Apostille Convention, Portuguese authorities do not accept Apostille stamps on Nepali documents. Instead, your Police Clearance Certificate (PCC), birth certificate, or marriage certificate must undergo a rigorous multi-stage consular legalization process across Kathmandu and New Delhi before AIMA or Portuguese consulates will accept them.

## Key Requirements / What Changed

The full legalization chain for Portugal (AIMA submission) is:

**Issuing Authority (Police HQ / Ward Office) → Nepal MOFA Attestation → Embassy of Nepal, New Delhi → Embassy of Portugal (via VFS Global, New Delhi)**

- **Strict PCC Validity:** AIMA requires your Police Clearance Certificate (PCC) to be recent—typically issued within the last **3 to 6 months**. Submitting an outdated criminal record check will lead to an immediate process suspension.
- **Mandatory Delhi Transit:** Portugal does not maintain a full diplomatic mission issuing consular legalizations in Kathmandu. Therefore, after Nepal MOFA attestation, your documents **must** go through the Embassy of Nepal in New Delhi before submission to the Portuguese Embassy via VFS Global Delhi.
- **Power of Attorney (POA):** If you are already residing in Portugal and cannot travel back to Asia, you can legally authorize a local agent or relative via a Power of Attorney to execute the entire process on your behalf.

## Step-by-Step Process

1. **Obtain Fresh Base Documents** — Download a fresh Police Clearance Certificate from the Nepal Police Online Portal, or retrieve recent civil status certificates (Birth/Marriage) from your local Ward Office.
2. **Nepal MOFA Attestation** — Apply online through the Department of Consular Services (NEATS portal) in Kathmandu, submit the original certificates, and receive the official QR-coded attestation sticker.
3. **Embassy of Nepal, New Delhi Verification** — Forward the MoFA-attested documents to New Delhi, where the Nepalese High Commission verifies the MoFA sticker's validity.
4. **Portugal Embassy Legalization (VFS Global New Delhi)** — Submit the documents to the VFS Portugal Application Centre in New Delhi for final legalization by the Portuguese Embassy consular desk.
5. **Express Courier to Portugal** — Once the Portuguese legalization stamp is applied, the documents are securely dispatched directly to your address in Portugal for your AIMA appointment.

**Estimated timeline:** roughly 25–35 working days end-to-end, depending primarily on VFS New Delhi submission slots and Portuguese Embassy processing times.

## Common Pitfalls

- Assuming an Apostille stamp is valid for Portugal on a Nepali document (Portugal accepts Apostille, but Nepal cannot issue them as a non-Hague member).
- Attempting to submit documents to VFS Portugal in Delhi without first receiving the Embassy of Nepal (New Delhi) verification stamp.
- Submitting a Police Clearance Certificate (PCC) that expires during the lengthy multi-embassy transit period.
