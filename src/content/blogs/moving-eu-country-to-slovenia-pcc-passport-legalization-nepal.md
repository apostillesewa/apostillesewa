## Introduction

For Nepali workers already living and working in the European Union — whether in Romania, Bulgaria, Croatia, or elsewhere — the idea of relocating within the EU to Slovenia can feel deceptively simple. After all, you're already inside the EU/Schengen system with a valid Temporary Residence Card (TRC). But Slovenia runs its own independent residence permit process for third-country nationals, and your existing TRC does not automatically transfer. Understanding this distinction early can save you weeks of delay.

## Key Requirements: What You Need Before Applying

Moving from an EU country to Slovenia as a Nepali national generally requires:

- **A valid, current TRC** in your existing EU country (Romania, Bulgaria, Croatia, etc.), proving continuous legal residence
- **A legalized Police Clearance Certificate (PCC)** and **legalized passport copy**, both authenticated through the **Embassy of Slovenia in New Delhi**
- **Purpose-of-stay documentation**, such as an employment contract, offer letter, or enrollment confirmation, depending on why you're moving

Since Slovenia does not maintain a diplomatic mission in Nepal, New Delhi is the accredited mission handling these legalizations for Nepali nationals.

## Step-by-Step Process

1. **Nepal Police (ePCC portal)** – Obtain your Police Clearance Certificate through Nepal's official ePCC system.
2. **Ministry of Foreign Affairs (MoFA), Tripureshwor** – Get the PCC attested with the MoFA sticker.
3. **Embassy of Slovenia, New Delhi** – Submit the PCC and a copy of your passport for final legalization.
4. **Confirm your TRC status** in your current EU country to ensure it remains valid throughout the application.
5. **Submit your application** to the relevant Slovenian diplomatic mission or administrative unit, along with your legalized documents and purpose-of-stay evidence.
6. **Await the residence permit decision** — processing timelines can vary, so plan ahead.
