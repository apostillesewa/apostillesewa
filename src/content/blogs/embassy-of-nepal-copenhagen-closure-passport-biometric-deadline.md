The **Embassy of Nepal in Copenhagen, Denmark** has officially announced that it is in the process of closing following a strategic decision by the Government of Nepal. 

This update directly affects Nepali citizens living in **Denmark** as well as all concurrently accredited countries: **Finland, Norway, Sweden, Estonia, Lithuania, Latvia, and Iceland**.

---

### 🚨 Urgent Deadline: Passport Biometrics

All service applicants residing in Denmark and the concurrently accredited Nordic and Baltic countries who need to apply for or renew their Nepali passport are urgently requested to attend the embassy in person for **biometric collection**.

* **Deadline for Biometrics:** **Bhadra 15, 2083 (August 31, 2026)**
* **Location:** Embassy of Nepal, Copenhagen, Denmark

*Applicants are strongly advised to complete their biometric submission prior to this deadline to prevent delays in passport issuance before full closure operations take effect.*

---

### ℹ️ Status of Essential Consular & Document Services

* **Consular Services:** Essential consular, verification, and legal services will continue to remain operational until further notice.
* **Emergency Assistance:** Citizens requiring urgent attestation or service inquiries during this transition phase should coordinate directly with the embassy promptly.
