## Introduction

If you are currently living in Europe, the UK, or anywhere abroad and suddenly need a fresh copy of your academic transcript, birth certificate, marriage certificate, or character certificate, flying back to Nepal is expensive and time-consuming. Whether your university in Poland or employer in Germany requires a recently issued document with a fresh MoFA stamp, you do not need to take leave or buy an expensive flight ticket to Kathmandu.

Nepali law allows your relatives or an authorized service provider to retrieve, re-issue, translate, and legalize official documents from local offices and ministries on your behalf. However, to ensure these documents pass strict foreign checks, you must follow the correct legal authorization chain.

## Key Requirements / What Changed

The full document retrieval and legalization chain is:

**Authorization / Power of Attorney → University / Ward Office / Police HQ → Law Book Management Board (Translation if needed) → Nepal MOFA Attestation → Destination Country Embassy Legalization**

- To retrieve original certificates or fresh copies (e.g., Tribhuvan University transcripts, Ward Office birth/marriage certificates, or Nepal Police Clearance Certificates), an **official authorization letter (Waris/Adhikarnama)** or signed consent is mandatory.
- Many foreign authorities (especially in the EU) require documents issued within the last **3 to 6 months** carrying a verifiable QR-coded **Nepal MoFA sticker**.
- English translations for court decrees or specific civil registry documents must be completed by the **Law Book Management Board (Kanoon Kitab Byabastha Samiti)** or certified translators—unverified private notary translations risk immediate rejection.

## Step-by-Step Process

1. **Issue Authorization** — Provide a signed authorization letter along with copies of your passport, citizenship (Nagarikta), and existing records so your representative can legally apply at the concerned authority (e.g., TU Exam Control Office, Ward Office, or Police HQ).
2. **Document Retrieval / Re-issuance** — Collect the fresh certificate, academic transcript, or civil status document directly from the issuing government office or educational institute in Nepal.
3. **Official English Translation** — If the newly issued document is solely in Nepali, have it officially translated through the Law Book Management Board or government-recognized channels.
4. **Nepal MOFA Attestation** — Apply online via the Department of Consular Services, book an attestation slot, and obtain the official QR-coded authentication sticker from the Kathmandu MoFA desk.
5. **Embassy / Consular Legalization** — Submit the MoFA-attested document to the relevant foreign embassy (in Kathmandu or New Delhi, depending on the destination country's consular setup) for final superlegalization.
6. **International Express Courier** — Dispatch the legalized documents safely to your doorstep in Europe or overseas.

**Estimated timeline:** roughly 10–20 working days end-to-end, depending on the issuing institution's processing speed and embassy appointment schedules.

## Common Pitfalls

- Submitting outdated civil certificates that foreign immigration (such as AIMA in Portugal or SIRI in Denmark) reject due to age limits.
- Attempting to retrieve university transcripts without providing the proper authorization documents or old registration details.
- Skipping official MoFA attestation on newly re-issued certificates before sending them abroad.
