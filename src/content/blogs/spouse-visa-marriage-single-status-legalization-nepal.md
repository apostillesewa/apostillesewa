## Introduction

If you are an NRN or Nepali worker living in Europe, the UK, or the Gulf and planning to bring your spouse via family reunification, document rejections are your biggest enemy. Over 80% of spouse visa delays and rejections occur not because of income or housing issues, but due to incorrectly attested Marriage Certificates or Single Status Certificates (Ekal Paripatra / Unmarried Certificate) from Nepal.

Because foreign immigration authorities (such as AIMA in Portugal, BAMF in Germany, or VFS centres) do not accept simple local documents, your civil status certificates must follow a strict, multi-stage authentication pipeline in Kathmandu and New Delhi before your spouse can apply for their visa.

## Key Requirements / What Changed

The mandatory legalization chain for family reunification and spouse visas is:

**Ward Office (Issuance) → Municipality Verification → Nepal MOFA Attestation → Destination Foreign Embassy Legalization**

- **Municipality Pre-Verification:** Skipping executive Municipality (Nagarpalika/Gaunpalika) verification after receiving your document from the Ward Office is the primary reason Nepal MOFA rejects Single Status and Marriage certificates.
- **English Letterhead & Photo:** Ward Offices must issue Unmarried Certificates on official English letterheads with the applicant's photograph, official Ward seal, and Ward Chairperson's signature.
- **Strict Validity Window:** Civil status certificates (especially Single Status) are usually considered expired by foreign embassies and European civil registries if they are older than **3 to 6 months**.

## Step-by-Step Process

1. **Document Issuance at Ward Office** — Obtain the official Marriage Registration Certificate or Single Status Certificate from your local Ward Office (Woda Karyalaya) of permanent residence.
2. **Municipal Level Endorsement** — Submit the document to the executive Municipal Office (Nagarpalika/Gaunpalika) to receive the Chief Administrative Officer's signature and municipal verification stamp.
3. **Nepal MOFA Online & Physical Attestation** — Log into the Consular Department (NEATS) portal, upload clear scans, select an attestation slot, and get the official QR-coded authentication sticker at Tripureshwor, Kathmandu.
4. **Official Law Book Translation (If Required)** — If the original certificate was issued purely in Nepali format, have it officially translated through the Law Book Management Board or government-certified translator prior to final submission.
5. **Destination Foreign Embassy Superlegalization** — Submit the MoFA-attested document to the destination country's embassy (located either in Kathmandu or New Delhi, depending on the country's consular coverage) for final legalization.

**Estimated timeline:** roughly 7–15 working days end-to-end, depending on municipality processing and embassy appointment queues.

## Common Pitfalls

- Using private notary translations instead of official bilingual Ward formats or Law Book Management Board translations.
- Submitting certificates to Nepal MoFA without the required executive Municipality verification stamp.
- Relying on Single Status Certificates issued over 6 months ago, resulting in immediate embassy rejection.
