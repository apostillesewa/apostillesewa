## Introduction

If you're sending Nepali documents abroad — for a work visa, study permit, or residency application in a country like Portugal, Poland, Spain, Italy, or elsewhere in Europe — there's a step most people don't budget for: attestation by the **Embassy of Nepal in New Delhi**. Many of the embassies that serve Nepali applicants are based in Delhi rather than Kathmandu, which means your documents need to travel there before the destination country's embassy will even look at them.

This guide breaks down the official 2026 fees, the required order of steps, and what else to budget for beyond the embassy counter.

## Key Requirements / What Changed

**Nepal Embassy attestation in New Delhi is typically required before submitting to:**
- Portugal Embassy
- Poland Embassy
- Spain Embassy
- Italy Embassy
- Czech Republic Embassy
- Slovakia Embassy
- Hungary Embassy
- Other European embassies located in New Delhi

**Official 2026 fees, by document type:**

| Document Type | Nepal Embassy Fee (INR) |
|---|---|
| Police Clearance Certificate (PCC) | INR 4,500 per document |
| Personal Documents (birth, marriage, divorce, single status, death, municipality/ward certificates) | INR 3,500 per document |
| Educational Documents (degree, transcript, character certificate, provisional certificate) | INR 3,500 per document |

**Prerequisite — MOFA Attestation (Nepal):** NPR 500 per document page, completed in Nepal before anything is sent to New Delhi. Without this step, the Nepal Embassy will generally not process your document.

## Step-by-Step Process

1. **Document Issued** — Obtain the original from the relevant issuing authority in Nepal.
2. **MOFA Attestation (Nepal)** — Submit to the Ministry of Foreign Affairs. Fee: NPR 500/page. Same-day processing.
3. **Nepal Embassy Verification (New Delhi)** — Submit the MOFA-attested document. Fee: INR 3,500–4,500 depending on type. Processing: ~2–3 hours.
4. **Foreign Embassy Legalization** — Submit the Nepal Embassy-attested document to the destination country's embassy in New Delhi.
5. **Document Ready for Use Abroad** — Your document is now legally recognized in the destination country.

**Beyond the official fees**, budget for: service charges, courier fees, translation (if required), Power of Attorney preparation, document handling, and the destination embassy's own legalization fee.
