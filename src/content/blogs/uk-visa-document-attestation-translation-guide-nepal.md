## Document Preparation for the United Kingdom

The United Kingdom continues to be one of the most sought-after destinations for Nepalese citizens. From students enrolling in prestigious British universities to healthcare professionals moving via the Health and Care Worker visa route, as well as families reuniting via dependent pathways, the influx of talent from Nepal to the UK is at an all-time high.

Because the UK is a strict signatory to the Hague Apostille Convention, their Visas and Immigration (UKVI) department enforces rigid verification standards for all foreign paperwork. 

Fortunately, unlike many European countries, **you do not need to send your documents to New Delhi for UK immigration purposes.** The UK accepts documents that have been officially authenticated right here in Kathmandu. However, the UKVI expects flawless execution regarding translations and mandatory medical clearances.

---

## The Core Standard: MoFA Attestation at Tripureshwor

To prove to British immigration officers that your certificates are genuine, all domestic Nepalese documents must go through a traditional, verified government chain:

1.  **Local/Line Ministry Verification:** Your academic transcripts must be authenticated by the National Examinations Board (NEB) or your university's controller of examinations, followed by the Department of Education. Vital certificates (birth, marriage, relationship) must carry a verified stamp from your local municipality or the Department of Civil Registration.
2.  **MoFA Attestation:** Once local departments sign off, your documents must be submitted to the Consular Services Department under the Ministry of Foreign Affairs (MoFA) in Tripureshwor, Kathmandu. MoFA applies the final, official state attestation sticker, making the document legitimate for international visa processing.

---

## Strict Rules for Certified English Translations

The UKVI will immediately reject any supporting document that is written in Nepali unless it is accompanied by a fully certified, professional English translation. 

The UK government does not accept casual or self-made translations. Every translated document—such as bank statements, property valuations, or birth certificates—must strictly include:
*   Confirmation from the translator that it is an accurate translation of the original document.
*   The date of the translation.
*   The translator's full name, signature, and official credentials/stamp.
*   The contact details of the translation company or professional.

Any mismatch between dates, numbers, or name spellings between your original Nepali certificate, its English translation, and your international passport will result in immediate non-compliance flags.

---

## The Mandatory Hurdle: The UK TB Test Clearance

If you are planning to stay in the UK for longer than 6 months (which applies to almost all student and work visas), you are legally required to undergo a Tuberculosis (TB) screening before submitting your visa application. 

This test cannot be done at just any local clinic or hospital. It must be performed at a UKVI-approved migration health center in Kathmandu—typically managed by the International Organization for Migration (IOM). The resulting TB clearance certificate is valid for 6 months and must be uploaded alongside your financial and academic portfolios.
