## The Delhi Embassy Advantage: Going the Extra Mile (Literally)

One of the biggest roadblocks for Nepalese citizens is that **many foreign embassies do not maintain diplomatic missions or consular offices inside Nepal**. Instead, their primary embassies for South Asia are located in New Delhi, India. 

Normally, this would mean you have to book expensive flights, arrange Indian visas, and spend days navigating Delhi's embassy lanes yourself. 

**Apostille Sewa eliminates this burden completely.** 

Through a legally secure **Power of Attorney (POA)**, our trained handling team safely travels to New Delhi on your behalf. We personally submit, track, and collect your documents directly from the respective embassies in India, bringing them back to Nepal fully authenticated and ready for your use.
