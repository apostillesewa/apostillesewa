## Introduction

If you've ever tried to get a document legalized for use abroad, you know the Ministry of Foreign Affairs (MoFA) Consular Services office in Tripureshwor, Kathmandu can feel like a maze. One counter handles birth certificates, another handles degrees, and a different set of rules applies depending on whether you're in Nepal right now or already living overseas. This guide breaks down exactly which documents go where, and what the process looks like for each category — whether you're applying in person or arranging it from abroad.

The MoFA Consular Section sits close to the Department of Passport in Tripureshwor, and in practice, most Nepali applicants dealing with international paperwork will end up passing through this office at some point.

## Key Requirements / What Changed

MoFA legalization requests generally fall into three categories, each with its own path:

### 1. Civil / Personal Documents
- Birth Certificate
- Relationship Certificate
- Marriage Certificate

These are normally issued at the **Ward Office**, then verified at the **Municipality Office**, before being submitted to MoFA for the final legalization stamp. However, if your document is **already digitally registered and verifiable within MoFA's own system**, this local ward-to-municipality step can sometimes be skipped, letting you go straight to MoFA.

### 2. Educational Documents
- Transcripts
- Degree Certificates
- Migration Certificates

These must originate from a recognized issuing body — the **National Examination Board (NEB)**, **SEE**, **Tribhuvan University (TU)**, **Pokhara University (PU)**, or your relevant board/university. MoFA will only legalize educational documents that carry proper certification from these recognized institutions.

### 3. Special-Purpose Documents
- **Medical Certificates**
- **Low-Income Certificates** — commonly required for need-based scholarship applications, such as Italy's need-based scholarship scheme
- **Passport Verification**

These are handled directly through MoFA's consular counters without necessarily needing the ward/municipality chain first, though supporting documentation requirements vary by case.

## Step-by-Step Process

1. **Identify your document category** — civil, educational, or special-purpose — since each has a different starting point.
2. **Complete local verification if required** — Ward Office and Municipality stamps for civil documents not already in MoFA's digital records.
3. **Confirm educational board certification** — ensure your transcript, degree, or migration certificate is properly issued and sealed by NEB, SEE, TU, PU, or the relevant board.
4. **Submit at MoFA Consular Services, Tripureshwor** — bring original documents, photocopies, a completed application form, and valid ID (citizenship or passport).
5. **If applying from abroad**, arrange submission through an authorized representative or with a Power of Attorney, since in-person presence in Kathmandu is not always required.
6. **Collect your legalized document** or arrange for it to be forwarded/couriered if you're overseas.
