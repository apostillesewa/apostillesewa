## Introduction

Transferring ownership of a four-wheeler or heavy commercial vehicle in Nepal involves specific legal requirements. Whether you are buying a private car, an electric SUV, a commercial bus, or heavy construction machinery like excavators and tippers, completing the official **Naamsari (ownership transfer)** is legally required. 

In Nepal, four-wheelers and heavy automobiles carry significantly higher tax liabilities, strict insurance rules, and mandatory route/fitness checks compared to two-wheelers. Moving forward with a sale on cash alone or holding an un-transferred vehicle leaves the seller liable for road accidents, unpaid traffic fines, and steep annual provincial taxes.

The cornerstone of this process is **Sanaakhat (legal verification)**—the formal in-person appearance where buyer and seller present valid identity documents and sign the official transport ledger before a Transport Officer. Here is the clear guide to navigating document requirements, tax rates, insurance costs, and step-by-step Naamsari for four-wheelers and heavy automobiles in Nepal.

---

## Required Documents: Buyer vs. Seller

Because cars, commercial trucks, and heavy vehicles involve greater legal liability, the document checklist depends on whether the vehicle is registered under an individual, a firm, or a corporate entity:

### 👤 Individual Ownership Checklist

* **Party A (Buyer / *Kharidkarta*):**
  * Original Citizenship Certificate (*Nagarikta*) + 2 photocopies.
  * 2 Recent passport-size photographs.
  * Duly completed Ownership Transfer Application Form (*Naamsari Form*).
* **Party B (Seller / *Vikrikarta*):**
  * Original Vehicle Registration Booklet (*Bluebook*).
  * Original Citizenship Certificate (*Nagarikta*) + 2 photocopies (for in-person *Sanaakhat* verification).

### 🏢 Company, Firm, or Commercial Vehicle Checklist

* **Company Documents:** Board Resolution Letter approving the vehicle sale/purchase, Certificate of Company Incorporation, Company PAN, and official company stamp.
* **Bank Loan Clearance (*Haq Chhod* Letter):** If the truck or car was financed, an official release letter with seal from the bank or financial institution is compulsory before Yatayat will process the transfer.
* **Commercial Fitness & Permit Certificates:** For buses, trucks, and tippers, bring the original **Route Permit (*Raut Parwana*)**, **Fitness Certificate (*Janch Bojh*)**, and **Pollution Clearance Pass (*Green Sticker*)**.

## Tax Rates & Third-Party Insurance Clearance

All past tax dues, road renewal fees, insurance policies, and traffic fines must be fully settled prior to initiating Naamsari.

### 1. Annual Four-Wheeler & Heavy Vehicle Tax Rates
Annual vehicle tax in Nepal varies by vehicle category, engine displacement (CC), or battery power (kW):

#### 🚗 Cars, Jeeps, Vans & Microbuses (Petrol / Diesel)
* **Up to 1000 cc:** NPR 22,000 / year
* **1001 cc – 1500 cc:** NPR 25,000 / year
* **1501 cc – 2000 cc:** NPR 27,000 / year
* **2001 cc – 2500 cc:** NPR 37,000 / year
* **2501 cc – 3000 cc:** NPR 50,000 / year
* **3001 cc – 3500 cc:** NPR 65,000 / year
* **Above 3501 cc:** NPR 70,000 / year
* **Annual Four-Wheeler Renewal Charge:** NPR 500.

#### ⚡ Electric Four-Wheelers (EV Cars & Vans)
* **10 kW – 50 kW:** NPR 5,000 / year
* **51 kW – 125 kW:** NPR 15,000 / year
* **126 kW – 200 kW:** NPR 20,000 / year
* **201 kW and Above:** NPR 30,000 / year.

#### 🚚 Trucks, Buses, Tractors & Heavy Construction Machinery
* **Tractors:** NPR 4,500 – NPR 6,500 / year (depending on use).
* **Mini Trucks / Mini Buses:** NPR 12,000 – NPR 16,000 / year.
* **Heavy Trucks, Tankers, Large Buses:** NPR 18,000 – NPR 25,000 / year.
* **Heavy Equipment (Excavators, Cranes, Dozers):** NPR 25,000 – NPR 40,000 / year depending on weight and category.

### 2. Mandatory Third-Party Insurance Policy
Standard Third-Party Motor Insurance rates for four-wheelers:
* **Up to 1000 cc:** NPR 7,365 / year
* **1001 cc – 1600 cc:** NPR 8,495 / year
* **Above 1601 cc:** NPR 10,747 / year
* **Heavy Commercial Vehicles & Trucks:** Calculated based on gross vehicle weight (GVW), carrying capacity, and seating configuration.

## Step-by-Step Naamsari & Sanaakhat Process


```

[Tax & Insurance Clearance] ➔ [Chassis & Engine Physical Inspection] ➔ [Sanaakhat Verification Desk] ➔ [Transfer Fee Payment] ➔ [New Bluebook Issuance]

```

### Step 1: Visit the Designated Yatayat Office
Both parties (or authorized legal representatives with power of attorney) must visit the specific **Transport Management Office (Four-Wheeler / Heavy Vehicle Division)** where the automobile is registered.

### Step 2: Revenue Settlement
Present the original Bluebook to verify that annual taxes, renewal charges, route permits, and any late fine penalties are fully paid.

### Step 3: Mechanical & Frame Physical Inspection
Drive the car, truck, or heavy equipment to the physical verification bay. A Yatayat vehicle inspector checks the physical **Chassis Number** and **Engine Number** against the Bluebook records. For commercial trucks and buses, fitness tests (*Janch Bojh*) are conducted.

### Step 4: The *Sanaakhat* Desk
Both buyer and seller appear before the Transport Officer:
* Original Citizenship certificates (*Nagarikta*) or official company identity cards are inspected.
* Both parties sign and place left and right thumbprints (*Laha Chhap*) on the official government transport register and transfer application.
* The Transport Officer approves and stamps the legal transfer (*Sanaakhat* completed).

### Step 5: Official Ownership Transfer Fee Payment
Pay the government transfer fee voucher at the cash desk:
* **Private Cars / SUVs:** NPR 1,500 – NPR 3,000 depending on vehicle class.
* **Heavy Commercial Trucks / Buses / Machinery:** NPR 3,000 – NPR 8,000 depending on tonnage and vehicle classification.

### Step 6: Updated Bluebook Collection
Submit the stamped files, payment receipts, and old Bluebook to the administrative counter. Yatayat prints the buyer's details on the official ownership ledger, applies government seals, and hands over the updated **Bluebook**.

## Key Considerations for Four-Wheelers & Heavy Equipment

* **Inter-Provincial Transfer Requirements:** If transferring a vehicle registered in one province (e.g., Bagmati) to a buyer residing in another (e.g., Gandaki), you must obtain a **No Objection Certificate (NOC / *Sifaris*)** from the home transport office before re-registering.
* **Commercial Vehicle Route Permits:** Commercial trucks and buses require updating the *Raut Parwana* (Route Permit) to reflect the new owner's name to avoid impoundment by traffic police.
* **Bank Release Stamp (*Haq Chhod*):** Ensure all previous finance liens are cancelled in the Yatayat database, not just recorded on paper.
