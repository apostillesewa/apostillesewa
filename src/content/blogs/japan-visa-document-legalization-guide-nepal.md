## Seamless Document Preparation for Japan

Japan remains one of the most prestigious destinations for Nepalese students, highly skilled IT professionals, and families moving on dependent visas. Whether you are aiming to study at a language school in Tokyo or starting a new corporate role, the Japanese immigration system is known worldwide for its precision, punctuality, and absolute attention to detail.

Unlike many European pathways where your paperwork must cross international borders to New Delhi, processing your documents for Japan happens right here in Kathmandu. The **Embassy of Japan is fully active in Panipokhari, Kathmandu**. 

However, because Japanese immigration reviews are incredibly strict, a single formatting mistake, a poor translation, or a stray staple can result in your entire visa file being rejected at the counter. Here is the definitive guide to preparing your documents flawlessly.

---

## The Core Rule: Strict Translation Policies

The Embassy of Japan will not evaluate documents submitted purely in the Nepali language. Any domestic certificate issued by local municipalities, courts, or banks must be officially translated into either **English** or **Japanese**.

This rule applies heavily to:
*   **Vital Statistics:** Birth Registration, Relationship Verification Certificates, and Marriage Certificates.
*   **Financial Proofs:** Bank Balance Certificates, 3-Year Bank Statements, and Tax Clearance Certificates from the Inland Revenue Department (IRD).
*   **Professional Credentials:** Certificate of Employment, business registration papers, and recommendation letters.

The translations must be executed by certified translation professionals who maintain the exact layout of the original document. Any discrepancy between dates, names, or numbers on the original Nepali document and the translated version will trigger immediate non-compliance flags.

---

## Strict Embassy Formatting Rules (The "No Staple" Policy)

While most countries focus only on the stamps on your paperwork, the Japanese Embassy and its processing partners place massive importance on physical presentation. When assembling your visa application file, you must adhere to these rigid structural guidelines:

*   **A4 Paper Size Only:** Every single document, certificate copy, and passport copy must be printed or copied on standard, clean A4 size paper. 
*   **No Staples or Paper Clips:** Do not use staples, paper pins, clips, or cello tape on your documents. The processing machinery reads pages sequentially, and physical binders can damage the sheets or delay the scanning process.
*   **Clear, Unblurred Photocopies:** Ensure all photocopies of your Certificate of Eligibility (COE), passport data pages, and citizenship certificates are completely sharp, readable, and free of dark ink lines.

---

## Understanding the Submission Path: VFS Global Kathmandu

To streamline operations, the Embassy of Japan utilizes **VFS Global** as its official outsourced partner for collecting visa applications and document files in Kathmandu. 

Once your local files are fully organized, translated, and formatted according to embassy standards, your complete file is submitted through the VFS Global Japan center. VFS performs the initial compliance check, securely handles your passport, and passes the certified file to the main Embassy in Panipokhari for final evaluation and visa stamping.
