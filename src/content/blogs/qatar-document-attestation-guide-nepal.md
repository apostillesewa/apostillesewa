## The Gateway to Doha: Understanding Qatar's Verification Standards

As Qatar shifts its economic focus toward high-end corporate infrastructure, international aviation, global hospitality, and advanced engineering, the profile of Nepalese professionals moving to Doha has evolved. Increasingly, high-tier technical specialists, corporate executives, and healthcare staff from Nepal are securing competitive contracts in the State of Qatar.

However, to claim your administrative or technical work visa, your domestic paperwork must undergo rigorous scrutiny. 

Fortunately, **you do not need to send your documents to New Delhi** for this process. The State of Qatar maintains a fully operational **Embassy in Maharajgunj, Kathmandu**. While the presence of a local embassy simplifies your logistics, the consular section enforces some of the strictest educational verification criteria in the Gulf region.

---

## The Ultimate Barrier: The "Regular Student" Degree Policy

If you are traveling on a professional-tier work visa that requires an academic degree, the Embassy of the State of Qatar enforces an uncompromised educational policy. 

**The Qatar Embassy will completely reject any degree obtained via distance learning, online education, open universities, or private/part-time enrollment.** 

To get your Bachelor’s, Master’s, or technical diploma attested, you must present an original, official **Verification Letter** from your university’s controller of examinations (e.g., Tribhuvan University, Kathmandu University, or NEB) explicitly stating:
1.  That you were a **regular, full-time student** who attended classes physically on campus.
2.  The exact duration of your study course and the enrollment dates.
3.  The type of degree obtained and the genuine authenticity of your transcript records.

Without this accompanying university letter, your academic degree will not receive the final embassy stamp, stalling your entire employment visa process.

## The Step-by-Step Document Attestation Chain

For your vital certificates (birth, marriage, relationship), online Police Clearance Certificates (PCC), or academic degrees to be legally accepted by Qatari immigration, they must climb an unbroken chain of physical stamps:

### Step 1: Institutional Verification
Your educational certificates must be verified by the National Examinations Board (NEB) or your specific university board, which must issue a transcript verification envelope. Personal documents must be authenticated by your local municipality or the Department of Civil Registration.

### Step 2: MoFA Attestation (Tripureshwor)
Take your verified certificates directly to the Consular Services Department under the Ministry of Foreign Affairs (MoFA) in Tripureshwor, Kathmandu. MoFA officials will review the local signatures and apply the official civil attestation sticker.

### Step 3: Qatar Embassy Legalization (Maharajgunj)
Once MoFA clears the file, it must be presented alongside your official Qatari Employment Contract and your university verification letter (for degrees) to the Consular Counter of the Qatar Embassy in Kathmandu to receive the final European-standard Gulf legalization seal.

---

## Document Attestation vs. The Qatar Visa Center (QVC)

A common point of confusion for new applicants is distinguishing between document attestation and the **Qatar Visa Center (QVC)** in Kathmandu. 

The QVC is a digital biometrics hub used for medical examinations, fingerprinting, and digital contract signing once your visa application is actively in progress. However, **the QVC does not handle the official attestation of your degrees, transcripts, or personal civil documents.** Those must be authenticated through the main Embassy channel in Maharajgunj prior to your final visa issuance phase.
